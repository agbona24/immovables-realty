# Property Images Guide

## 📁 Upload Your Property Banners Here

Place your property banner images in the `properties/` folder with the exact filenames below:

### Required Image Files:

1. **prime-haven-grove.jpg** - Prime Haven Grove Estate
2. **wura-gardens.jpg** - Wura Gardens Estate
3. **prime-haven-4br.jpg** - Prime Haven Groven 4BR Detached
4. **prime-haven-semi.jpg** - Prime Haven Groven Semi-Detached
5. **olomore-house.jpg** - House in Olomore
6. **3-units.jpg** - 3 Units (2BR Flats + Self-Con)
7. **blocks-of-flats.jpg** - Blocks of Flats - Prime Area
8. **gbokoniyi-plots.jpg** - 3 Plots at Gbokoniyi
9. **2-plots.jpg** - 2 Plots for Sale

---

## 📐 Image Specifications:

- **Format:** JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **Recommended Size:** 1200x800px (3:2 aspect ratio)
- **Maximum File Size:** 2MB per image
- **Quality:** High resolution for sharp display on all devices

---

## ✅ How to Upload:

1. Rename your property banners to match the exact filenames above
2. Upload them to: `/public/images/properties/`
3. The website will automatically display them!

**Note:** If images are not uploaded yet, beautiful gradient backgrounds will be shown as placeholders.

---

## 🎨 Image Tips:

- Use high-quality, professional photos
- Show the best angles of each property
- Ensure good lighting and clarity
- Include property signage if available
- Avoid watermarks that obscure the property
